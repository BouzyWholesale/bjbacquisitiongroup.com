About Us

BJB Acquisition Group is a Florida-based land acquisition company founded by brothers Ysvelt Bouzy and Berny Bouzy. Our mission is to create win-win opportunities for both property owners and investors by simplifying the land acquisition process.

We understand that selling land can often be time-consuming and complicated. That's why we focus on providing fair offers, transparent communication, and efficient closings. For investors, we source quality land opportunities throughout Florida that have strong potential for future growth and development.

As a family-owned business, integrity, professionalism, and long-term relationships are at the center of everything we do.
